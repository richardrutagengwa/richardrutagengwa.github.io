Professional Profile

Digital systems designed for real public-sector needs

I am a Digital Health and Health Information Systems Technical Advisor based in Kigali, Rwanda, with experience across health data management, software development, information-system implementation and public-sector digital transformation.

At HISP Rwanda, I help translate programme and service-delivery needs into practical digital solutions. My work includes DHIS2 configuration, workflow design, testing, dashboards and data triangulation, deployment support, and training for national and multi-country initiatives.

My portfolio spans disease surveillance, immunization, electronic medical records, climate and health, and education information systems. I bring together technical implementation, data quality, stakeholder collaboration and capacity development so that systems are usable by the people who rely on them and can support better decisions.

01

National Event Management System (EMS) — Rwanda

Concept illustration · Open related HISP Rwanda story ↗

Contributed to a national One Health event-management platform intended to connect human and animal eIDSR, Impuruza event-based surveillance and EIOS open-source signals. The system design includes configurable workflows, threshold monitoring, analytics, dashboards and epidemiological bulletins aligned with the 7-1-7 framework.

Documented outputs: system architecture and integration design, program rules, dashboard and threshold-monitoring prototypes, weekly bulletin functionality, user acceptance activities and technical-committee pilot validation. Deployment status: production rollout was still pending server availability in the April 2026 report.

Capacity building: the report records 50 participants in an EMS training, 50 of 56 hospital IDSR focal persons attending a separate session, and a workshop involving directors from 56 hospitals.

Practical value: brings immunisation indicators and data-quality signals into a shared review process, helping district and subdistrict teams identify gaps and prioritise follow-up.
