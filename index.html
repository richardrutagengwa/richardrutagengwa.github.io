Professional Profile

Digital systems designed for real public-sector needs

I am a Digital Health and Health Information Systems Technical Advisor based in Kigali, Rwanda, with experience across health data management, software development, information-system implementation and public-sector digital transformation.

At HISP Rwanda, I help translate programme and service-delivery needs into practical digital solutions. My work includes DHIS2 configuration, workflow design, testing, dashboards and data triangulation, deployment support, and training for national and multi-country initiatives.

My portfolio spans disease surveillance, immunization, electronic medical records, climate and health, and education information systems. I bring together technical implementation, data quality, stakeholder collaboration and capacity development so that systems are usable by the people who rely on them and can support better decisions.
